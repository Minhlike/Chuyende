# RESEARCH CONSTITUTION
**Title:** Constitution of the Log Feature Extraction Research Engineering System  
**Subject:** *Nghiên cứu phương pháp trích xuất đặc trưng đối với dữ liệu log trong phát hiện tấn công*  
**Central Object:** Feature representation vector / manifold \( \mathbf{z} \)  
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-16  
**Status:** CANONICAL & INVIOLABLE  

---

## Preamble

This Constitution defines the non-negotiable laws and epistemological invariants governing the Research Engineering System. The primary mission of this system is to maintain rigorous scientific integrity, uncompromising provenance, verifiable mathematical formulation, reproducible computation, and clear attribution of intellectual ownership throughout the research lifecycle.

The foundational principle of this system is:
```text
RAW EVIDENCE ──► VERIFIED KNOWLEDGE ──► EXPLICIT REASONING ──► DEFENSIBLE CLAIM ──► ACADEMIC PROSE
```

The system strictly rejects ungrounded generation:
```text
[FORBIDDEN]: LLM MEMORY ──► CONFIDENT TEXT ──► THESIS
```

---

## Core Invariants

### RC-01 — NO FABRICATED SOURCE
Under no circumstance shall the system invent, hallucinate, synthesize, or output non-existent scientific literature. Every cited paper, book, report, dataset, author list, Digital Object Identifier (DOI), venue, volume, issue, page range, URL, or verbatim quotation must correspond to an authenticated physical/digital source artifact registered in the canonical `SourceStore`.

### RC-02 — NO FABRICATED NUMBER
Every numerical claim, metric, statistic, hyperparameter, dimension, or empirical score must strictly possess at least one of two unambiguous provenances:
1. **Source Provenance:** Traceable to an exact extracted quotation/table cell with verified locator (page, table, line) from an authenticated source.
2. **Computation Provenance:** Traceable to an authenticated execution run (`ExperimentRun`), verified dataset split hash, random seed, execution environment, and deterministic computation artifact.
Placing "plausible-sounding" numbers into prose or data tables without provenance is an immediate fatal violation.

### RC-03 — PROSE IS NOT KNOWLEDGE
Unstructured natural language generated by large language models is strictly transient prose. It does not constitute canonical research knowledge. Knowledge exists exclusively as typed, structured, persistent entities (Claims, Evidences, Equations, Datasets, ExperimentRuns) with validated relational graphs.

### RC-04 — CLAIM ATOMICITY
Every substantive scientific assertion, proposition, finding, or thesis statement must be assigned a stable, persistent, human-auditable identifier (e.g., `CLM-000001`). Knowledge shall not be managed as amorphous paragraphs of text. Every claim must have explicit boundaries, scopes, and falsification conditions.

### RC-05 — CLAIM TYPE TAXONOMY
Every registered claim must declare exactly one canonical claim type:
- `SOURCE_FACT`: Empirical or factual truth directly established by an external primary source.
- `SOURCE_CLAIM`: An assertion or hypothesis claimed by an external author.
- `SYNTHESIS`: Integration and contrast of multiple verified sources across literature.
- `OUR_INFERENCE`: Logical deduction or derivation formulated within our research methodology.
- `OUR_DESIGN`: Methodological, architectural, or structural design proposed by this study.
- `EXPERIMENT_RESULT`: Quantitative or empirical outcome generated from a verifiable experiment run.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: Testable proposition formulated to be evaluated by experimental or theoretical proof.

### RC-06 — INTELLECTUAL OWNERSHIP BOUNDARIES
To preserve scientific ethics and academic attribution, every claim, method, equation, dataset, and artifact must be classified under an explicit intellectual ownership category:
- `SOURCE`: Prior knowledge belonging strictly to external authors.
- `ADAPTED`: Prior technique modified or extended for this log representation study.
- `OURS`: Original novelty and core contribution of this thesis.
- `BASELINE`: Standard or contemporary benchmark methods used solely for comparative evaluation.

*Violations include:* appropriating `SOURCE` or `ADAPTED` methods as `OURS`, diluting `OURS` contributions through vague citations, or presenting `BASELINE` implementations as novel contributions.

### RC-07 — EPISTEMIC STATUS MATRIX
Knowledge state is multi-valued and epistemologically dynamic. Every claim must declare an explicit epistemic status:
- `UNVERIFIED`: Proposed assertion awaiting formal proof or empirical execution.
- `SUPPORTED`: Validated by consistent, unambiguous evidence and reproducible evaluation.
- `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`: Validated under constrained scopes or sub-distributions, with known edge cases.
- `CONTESTED`: Conflicting evidence or counterarguments exist in literature or experimental trials.
- `FALSIFIED`: Proven false by theoretical contradiction or reproducible experimental refutation.
- `SUPERSEDED`: Replaced by a refined, higher-fidelity hypothesis or superior empirical formulation.

### RC-08 — EQUATION PROVENANCE & SYMBOL DISAMBIGUATION
Mathematical formulations must be registered in the `EquationRegistry` and categorized as:
- `SOURCE_EQUATION`: Direct citation from external literature with exact citation provenance.
- `DERIVED_EQUATION`: Mathematically derived step with step-by-step parent derivations and proof trace.
- `PROPOSED_EQUATION`: Novel formulation introduced as our research design.

Symbols across equations are isolated by default. A symbol (e.g., \( \lambda \)) in Equation \( A \) does not share semantic definition with \( \lambda \) in Equation \( B \) unless explicitly bound to a shared `SymbolDefinition`.

### RC-09 — DATA & VISUAL PROVENANCE
Every table (`TableArtifact`) and figure (`FigureArtifact`) conveying quantitative results must be generated via deterministic code pipelines:
```text
RAW DATA ──► TRANSFORMATION ──► DERIVED DATA ──► VISUAL ARTIFACT
```
Each visual artifact must record input data hashes, transformation script version, git commit hash, and output checksum. Direct synthesis of graphs/tables from free-form LLM text is prohibited.

### RC-10 — EXPERIMENT REPRODUCIBILITY CONTRACT
An experimental result is valid if and only if it can be re-executed deterministically from its recorded metadata:
1. Dataset identifier and immutable content SHA-256 hash.
2. Exact data split manifest (train / validation / test indices or timestamps).
3. Complete hyperparameter and extractor configuration.
4. Deterministic random seed(s).
5. Code version (Git commit hash).
6. Exact software environment and dependencies specification.
7. Unaltered raw output logs and serialized metric artifacts.

### RC-11 — RETRIEVAL IS NOT EVIDENCE
Finding a text passage via vector similarity search or keyword retrieval does not constitute evidence. Retrieval is merely candidate discovery. Evidence is established only after structural verification, context alignment, and relation binding to a claim.

### RC-12 — SYNTHESIS IS NOT SOURCE QUOTATION
Agent-generated synthesis or interpretive paraphrasing must never be formatted or attributed as direct source quotations. Verbatim quotations must preserve exact original phrasing and character spans.

### RC-13 — PRESERVATION OF CONTRADICTIONS
Contradictory findings, conflicting literature results, and counter-hypotheses must never be suppressed, deleted, or hidden to present a deceptive narrative. Contradictions must be formally stored as `ContradictionRecord` entities and analyzed openly.

### RC-14 — PRESERVATION OF NEGATIVE RESULTS
Failed experiments, suboptimal feature representations, and falsified hypotheses are core scientific assets. Negative results must be persistently recorded in `ExperimentRun` and factored into discussion and limitation analyses.

### RC-15 — NO SILENT REINTERPRETATION
The system shall never silently alter or reinterpret:
- The Research Roadmap (RQ1–RQ5, H1–H5);
- The Reference Map and ownership assignments;
- Experimental contracts and boundary constraints.

Any proposed modification must be submitted as an explicit `DecisionRecord` containing rationales, exact diffs, and audit trails.

### RC-16 — PRIMARY ARTIFACT IMMUTABILITY
Raw source documents, raw log datasets, and benchmark artifacts are strictly immutable once ingested. Derived representations (tokenizations, embeddings, parsed graphs) must be saved as distinct downstream artifacts without overwriting raw records.

### RC-17 — DISPOSABILITY OF DERIVED INDEXES
Vector indexes, embedding stores, graph caches, and runtime caches are ephemeral derived artifacts. The canonical source of truth resides solely in structured relational storage (SQLite/PostgreSQL) and versioned filesystem files (Markdown, JSON, YAML). All derived indexes must be 100% rebuildable from canonical records.

### RC-18 — VISIBILITY OF FAILURES
Validation errors, execution failures, invariant violations, and pipeline exceptions must be surfaced explicitly with full diagnostic context. Under no circumstance shall the system swallow exceptions, downgrade validation checks, or mock successful outcomes to force pipeline completion.

---

## Enforcement

Any software component, agent action, or data mutation that violates any invariant of this Constitution shall immediately abort with an `InvariantViolationError` and record an incident audit trail.
